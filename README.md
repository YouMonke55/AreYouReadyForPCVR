# Welcome

This guide is intended to help players set up their VR headset for PCVR before playing **ASCENTVR** or any of my future PCVR games.

Whether you're using a Meta Quest, Valve Index, HTC Vive, Oculus Rift, or Pico headset, this guide should help you get connected.

> **Note:** I personally develop and test using a Meta Quest 2 over PCVR. Some information for other headsets is based on official documentation and community knowledge, so if you notice anything incorrect, please let me know.

---

# Minimum Requirements

To play PCVR games, you will generally need:

* Windows 10 or Windows 11, Recommened Windows 11 for new/updated Windows compatablity
* A VR Ready PC
* A supported PCVR compatable headset
* Steam
* SteamVR
* Enough storage space for your games, Recommened SSD space for fast loading

---

# Installing SteamVR

1. Install Steam.
2. Search for **SteamVR**.
3. Install SteamVR.
4. Launch SteamVR.

If SteamVR opens successfully, you're ready for the next step.

---

# Meta Quest Setup

Supported:

* Quest
* Quest 2
* Quest 3
* Quest 3S

### Option 1 - Meta Horizon Link (Recommended)

1. Install Meta Horizon Link on your PC.
2. Connect your headset using a USB cable or Wi-Fi.
3. Launch Meta Horizon Link.
4. Connect to your PC.
5. Launch SteamVR.

### Option 2 - Virtual Desktop

1. Purchase Virtual Desktop from the Meta Store.
2. Install the Virtual Desktop Streamer on your PC.
3. Connect your headset.
4. Launch SteamVR.

---

# Valve Index Setup

1. Connect the headset.
2. Connect the DisplayPort cable.
3. Connect USB.
4. Connect power.
5. Turn on your Base Stations.
6. Turn on your controllers.
7. Launch SteamVR.

If everything appears green inside SteamVR, your headset is ready.

---

# HTC Vive Setup

1. Connect your Link Box.
2. Connect your headset.
3. Turn on your Base Stations.
4. Turn on your controllers.
5. Launch SteamVR.

---

# Oculus Rift / Rift S

1. Install Meta Horizon Link.
2. Complete the headset setup.
3. Launch SteamVR.

---

# Pico 4 / Pico 4 Ultra

Use Pico's official PC streaming software to connect your headset.

Once connected:

* Launch SteamVR
* Wait for your headset to appear
* Start your game

# Wired vs Wireless

## Wired

Pros

* Stable connection
* Lowest latency
* Better image quality

Cons

* Cable can get in the way

---

## Wireless

Pros

* No cable
* Easier to move around

Cons

* Requires a strong Wi-Fi connection
* May have additional latency

# Is My PC Powerful Enough?

If your PC already plays other PCVR games without major issues, it should be able to run ASCENTVR.
Please do not compare a Desktop game to a Virtual Reality game as VR requires more CPU, GPU and Memory Power than regular Desktop.

Examples include:

* Boneworks
* BONELAB
* Half-Life: Alyx
* Beat Saber
* Arizona Sunshine 2

Performance will depend on your hardware and graphics settings.

# Troubleshooting

## SteamVR won't open

* Restart Steam.
* Restart your PC.
* Check headset connections.
* Update headset drivers.

---

## Headset not detected

* Reconnect the USB cable.
* Try another USB port.
* Restart SteamVR.
* Restart the headset.

---

## Controllers won't connect

* Replace or recharge batteries (if applicable).
* Pair them again.
* Restart SteamVR.

---

## Black screen

* Check DisplayPort or USB connections.
* Restart your headset.
* Restart SteamVR.

---

## Low FPS

* Lower graphics settings.
* Close background applications.
* Update GPU drivers.
* Restart SteamVR.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I play without a VR headset?

No.

ASCENTVR is a PCVR-only experience.

---

## Does it support standalone Quest?

No.

A VR-ready PC is required.

---

## Does Virtual Desktop work?

Yes.

Virtual Desktop should work just like Meta Horizon Link.

---

## Does Air Link work?

Yes.

As long as your network is stable.

---

## Does Wi-Fi work?

Yes.

A 5 GHz or faster Wi-Fi network is recommended.

---

## Can I use a cable instead?

Yes.

USB Link generally provides the most stable experience.
Highly recommend 5Gbps/USB 3.0 cable a good (but maybe expensive) can be found on the official META website for around 100 USD/80 GBP.

---

## Which headset is recommended?

I personally develop using a Quest 2 connected to my PC, so that is the headset I have the most experience supporting.
But any other headset (newer or not to much older) should work.
